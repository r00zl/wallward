How to play

  1. A foe starts 12 paces away and steps closer every few seconds.
  2. Type the answer and press Enter. A true answer sends the knight in to strike, and drives the foe back 3 paces.
  3. Three true answers fell it. Small spelling slips are forgiven.
  4. A wrong answer puts out one of your five torches and brings a new question. So does letting the foe reach you.
  5. As a foe draws near, letters of the answer are revealed, though never enough to give it away.

Today's wall has five foes and is the same for everyone. It changes at 00:00 UTC. Answer quickly and lose no torch for the most glory: 10,000 for a perfect wall.
