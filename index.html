    How to play

    1. Each foe asks for a name from history, such as a Roman emperor. Any true answer strikes it. Small spelling slips are forgiven.
    2. Give the oldest answer you can. The older it is, the harder the blow: 1, 2 or 3 damage. You only learn which when it lands.
    3. The oldest answer of all fells any foe outright, whatever its health. So does one a hair younger.
    4. Keep giving new names until its health is gone. Every blow drives it back and buys you time.
    5. True answers in a row build a combo. Each one is worth more glory, and the knight strikes faster and harder.
    6. A wrong name lets the foe gain a pace and breaks the combo. If it reaches you, one of your five torches goes out.
    7. Out of names? Skip the foe. It costs a torch, and you keep the glory for the damage done.

    Today's wall has five foes and is the same for everyone. It changes at 00:00 UTC. Every oldest answer you find is kept in your Almanac.
