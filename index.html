How to play

  1. An enemy starts 12 paces away and steps closer every few seconds.
  2. Type the answer and press Enter to shoot. Small spelling slips are forgiven.
  3. Each hit drives it back 2 paces. Three correct answers in a row fell it.
  4. A wrong answer breaks your chain and lets it lunge a pace closer. Skipping (or typing ?) breaks the chain but costs no ground.
  5. If it reaches you, a lantern goes out. Lose all three and the wall falls.

Today's wall has five enemies and is the same for everyone. It changes at 00:00 UTC. A fast, flawless wall is worth 10,000 li, the legendary length of the Great Wall.
