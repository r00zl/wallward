    How to play

    1. Each foe asks for a name: a Roman emperor, say, or a video game. Any true answer strikes it. Small spelling slips are forgiven.
    2. Give the oldest answer you can. The older it is, the harder the blow: 1, 2 or 3 damage. You only learn which when it lands.
    3. The oldest answer of all fells any foe outright, whatever its health. So does one a hair younger.
    4. A true answer the game does not list still counts for painters, writers, battles, video games and the like. It is checked against Wikidata, which is sent only the name you typed.
    5. Keep giving new names until its health is gone. Every blow drives it back and buys you time.
    6. True answers in a row build a combo. Each one is worth more glory, and the knight strikes faster and harder.
    7. A wrong name lets the foe gain a pace and breaks the combo. If it reaches you, one of your five torches goes out.
    8. Out of names? Skip the foe. It costs a torch, and you keep the glory for the damage done.

    Today's wall has five foes and is the same for everyone. It changes at 00:00 UTC. Every oldest answer you find is kept in your Almanac, and enough of them win the knight new looks.

    The foes do not wait. Hide or close the page and they keep coming: a torch goes out each time one reaches you, whether you are there or not.
